# Invitation to Review: the RE-SETI Concept

Dear colleague,

I am looking for a small number of thoughtful people to give an early-stage idea a sanity review before any further commitment is made.

## The idea, in one paragraph

Existing radio SETI surveys, including SETI@home and Breakthrough Listen, produced large datasets but no confirmed technosignatures. Modern AI pattern-recognition methods have advanced considerably since much of that data was first analyzed. The proposal is a carefully scoped reanalysis of publicly available or previously processed radio data using present-day anomaly-detection and clustering techniques, under an explicit "trust but verify" protocol that requires synthetic-signal injection, independent confirmation where possible, and human review of any high-ranking candidates. The goal is not to claim a discovery, but to find out whether today's methods would surface anything the earlier pipelines missed, to document the outcome either way, and to leave behind benchmark data that future surveys can use.

RE-SETI is an independent proposal. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by UC Berkeley or its SETI@home project, Breakthrough Listen, or the SETI Institute.

## What I am asking of you

A critical reading of a short briefing note and, if you wish, the longer proposal archive. I am particularly interested in whether the scientific premise is reasonable, whether the validation approach is adequate, what major risks or blind spots exist, and whether the framing is clear without overclaiming. Negative or skeptical feedback is as useful as positive feedback.

## What this is not

- Not a request for funding, a time commitment, or co-founding.
- Not an operational project yet. No organization exists and no infrastructure has been built. The proposal materials are posted publicly for transparency, but no launch or formal announcement is planned until after review.
- Not dependent on your endorsement for me to continue thinking about it.
