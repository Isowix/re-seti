# About the author

*RE-SETI proposal archive v1.3 · 24 November 2026*

**Phil Rail** is the owner of Milford Machining & Fabrication, based in Picton, Ontario. He has more than ten years of experience in CAD, machining, industrial design, fabrication and automation, and works in computational design with PicoGK and LEAP 71 tools.

RE-SETI is his independent proposal. He has no academic affiliation, and no formal credentials in radio astronomy or in signal processing. Those are real gaps. The project is written so that an expert in those areas can take the methods, the pilot and the data plan and put them on a firmer footing. Reviewers and collaborators with that background are invited to do so.

RE-SETI is not a project of Milford Machining & Fabrication, of UC Berkeley, of Breakthrough Listen, or of the SETI Institute, and it should not be read as implying any of those affiliations.

Contact: hello@axiom-mmf.ca, and https://github.com/Isowix/re-seti/issues (public).
