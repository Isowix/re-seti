# Statement of Intent

*RE-SETI proposal archive v1.3 · 24 November 2026 · Prepared by Phil Rail*

> **Stage: proposal in review.** Seeking reviewers and scientific or technical collaborators, not a business co-founder.

RE-SETI is an independent proposal. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by UC Berkeley or its SETI@home project, the Berkeley SETI Research Center, Breakthrough Listen or the Breakthrough Initiatives, or the SETI Institute.

If this proposal goes ahead, it will be run according to the following commitments.

1. **Reanalysis, not new claims.** The project will re-examine radio SETI data that has already been recorded. It will not operate telescopes or collect new data.
2. **Trust but verify.** Completeness is measured by injecting test signals before flagging and detection, not after. Automated detection is followed by a registered false-discovery-rate rule, archival cross-match where the data allow, and human review. New telescope observations are out of scope until a partner exists. No discovery will be announced without independent confirmation.
3. **Measured, honest results.** Sensitivity will be measured with injected synthetic signals and published. A null result will be reported with the same care as any positive one.
4. **Respect for earlier work.** The project builds on SETI@home, Breakthrough Listen and others. Revisiting their data is not a claim that their work was flawed; it is a test of what newer methods add.
5. **Open by default.** Methods, code, trained models and benchmark datasets will be published under open licences, except where data agreements require otherwise.
6. **Vendor-neutral.** The project will not depend on any single AI model, cloud provider or company.
7. **Respect for volunteers.** Volunteer software will be sandboxed, transparent about what it runs, and easy to remove. Volunteers' contributions will be acknowledged.
8. **Data custodians first.** Data that is not public will be used only with the agreement of its custodians, and on their terms.

— Phil Rail, proposer
